How to play

Create a room and share its code. At least 2 connected players are needed to start. Everyone acts at the same time, but each player can handle only one task at a time.

Use the care buttons to refill Oxygen, Food, Rest, and Health. Care takes 4 seconds; repairs take 6. A claimed task shows your teammate’s name. Other tasks remain available.

Gus wakes after 2:30. Fix ship alerts to stop extra drain. Empty Oxygen, Food, or Rest damages Health. Survive 5 minutes to win; anyone reaching zero Health ends the mission.

Care that restores a meter earns 10 points, repairs earn 50, and surviving earns 500. Players can join during a mission. If someone disconnects, their task is released.
